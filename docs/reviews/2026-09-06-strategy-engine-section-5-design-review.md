# Design review — Strategy Engine plan §5

Reviewed under `docs/EXPERIMENT_REVIEW_PROTOCOL.md` and HANDOFF §0T.

Review target: the original plan at `1472035`, including the first Codex contract amendment at
`62618e7`. Plan author: Claude. Design challenger: Codex. This is a design/protocol review; no
backtest was run and no empirical result is endorsed here.

## Verdict

**REJECT AS WRITTEN at L2: numeric scoring and score-band implementation must not begin.**

The allowed next design is **boolean-first** candidate evaluation. Phase 2A may record eligibility,
direction, rejection reasons and frozen signal-time features with `score = null`. A score may be
researched later in shadow mode, but it cannot gate a signal until it beats the boolean baseline on
untouched forward/OOS data after multiplicity correction and independent review.

This verdict does not reject the isolated Phase 1 context engines in `62618e7`; they do not score,
filter, write signals or reach Telegram. It does reject the plan's implication that six hand-assigned
weights and five score labels are ready to become executable policy.

## D1 — the six weights have no earned advantage

HANDOFF §5.19 records why a hand-written scalar is not evidence merely because it ranges from 0 to 1.
For the three largest rule cohorts, legacy `corr(confidence, R)` was 0.013, 0.051 and 0.027; the repo
therefore already forbids using that value as a filter. A multifeature score *could* contain more
information, but assigning 25/20/15/20/10/10 by hand supplies no evidence that it does.

Decision:

1. The six proposed weights remain explanatory notes only. They are not seeded into
   `strategy_versions`, used to rank candidates or mapped to NO_TRADE…A+.
2. Phase 2A uses a versioned boolean policy: required context is present, location is eligible,
   direction agrees and the strategy's required detector set is satisfied. It logs every failure by
   a closed rejection reason.
3. If a later Phase 2B proposes a score, its pre-registration must include three baselines:
   eligibility-only boolean policy, equal-weight/evidence-count policy, and legacy confidence. The
   primary estimand is the after-cost incremental result of the frozen score-gated policy over the
   same opportunities generated by the boolean policy—not correlation on surviving trades.
4. Each component needs an OOS ablation. If the weighted policy does not improve the primary estimand
   after adjusted uncertainty, it is rejected; a visually attractive ranking is not a fallback win.

Thus the answer to item 1 is: **the weights are not expected or allowed to succeed by assumption;
they must earn incremental value, and until then no numeric score exists.**

## D2 — all three strategies are one hypothesis family

`NQ_PULLBACK_V1`, `NQ_REVERSAL_V1` and `GC_SWEEP_V1` are evaluated on the same historical programme
and share bars/features. Reporting the best unadjusted result would be selection over three chances.

Decision:

- Register exactly one primary policy contrast per strategy before outcomes are read. Those three
  contrasts form one family with family-wise alpha 0.05.
- Report raw and Holm-adjusted p-values/confidence decisions for all three. Holm controls FWER under
  arbitrary dependence; no claim may use an unadjusted per-strategy threshold.
- Resample the three contrasts jointly with the same session × instrument block draws. This preserves
  within-session and cross-strategy dependence rather than pretending candidates are IID trades.
- Direction, session, regime, band and component-ablation slices are descriptive secondary analyses.
  Any slice promoted to a confirmatory claim joins a newly registered family and consumes its error
  budget; it may not inherit the original three-test decision.
- Selecting the best development strategy requires an untouched final OOS interval. The selected
  winner cannot certify itself on the data that selected it.

## D3 — no sample-size gate, no score-band verdict

An A+ label on five observations is typography, not evidence. The fix is not another arbitrary
`n >= constant`; it is a frozen power contract tied to the economic question.

Before reading Phase 2 performance outcomes, the experiment row must freeze:

- the primary estimand: after-cost incremental R per boolean-eligible opportunity versus the frozen
  boolean policy;
- the owner-selected smallest economically relevant effect (SESOI), including commission, slippage
  and missed-fill assumptions;
- family alpha 0.05 with the conservative planning threshold `0.05 / 3`, target power at least 0.80,
  and session × instrument as the resampling unit;
- the development, walk-forward and final OOS boundaries plus the minimum number of independent
  blocks and opportunities produced by that power calculation;
- pass/fail handling for incomplete blocks, missing outcomes and informative censoring.

Until all gates pass, results are `ENGINEERING_ONLY / UNDERPOWERED`: candidate logging and deterministic
formula checks may continue, but no “best strategy”, band-quality, edge or production statement is
allowed. Empty/small A+ cells are not pooled post hoc and band boundaries are not widened after seeing
counts. This directly prevents a repeat of the V4 situation where code readiness was mistaken for an
answer before the pre-registered information gate opened.

## D4 — boolean-first is the chosen contract

The phases are now explicit:

| Stage | Output | May affect live signals? | Exit condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| **2A boolean** | `eligible`, direction, rejection reasons, raw frozen features; `score = null`, `classification = null` | No; candidate table only | deterministic tests + independent review |
| **2B score shadow** | frozen component transforms/contributions and numeric score, still non-actionable | No | Gate 0, adequate power, walk-forward and final OOS advantage over 2A after D2 correction |
| **2C score gate** | score band may participate in acceptance | Only after written owner approval | independent raw re-run, forward evidence, rollback and L3 approval |

The database contract must encode the stage. A nullable score is not an implementation omission:
`decision_mode = 'boolean'` requires score/classification to be null; `score_shadow` stores them but
cannot accept/reject a live signal; only a separately approved `score_gate` version may use them.
